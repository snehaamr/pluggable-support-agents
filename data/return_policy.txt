RETURN POLICY

Membership tiers
- Gold: 30-day return window from the delivery date. Refund is 100% of the purchase price.
- Silver: 20-day return window from the delivery date. Refund is 75% of the purchase price.
- Bronze: 15-day return window from the delivery date. The refund amount depends on product condition and is not issued automatically.

Eligible order statuses
- delivered
- shipped
- return_requested

Damage and defects
- Damaged or defective items need a damage note before a refund can be issued.

Refund timing
- Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5 days.
